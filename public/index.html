How FoodSave AI works

  1. Business lists surplus
    Food, quantity, price, pickup window and storage info.
  2. AI suggests a discount
    Category and price recommendations. The business decides.
  3. Nearby people are alerted
    Only opted-in customers inside their chosen radius.
  4. Reserve and collect
    Pay less, pick up before the deadline with a code.
  5. Or donate
    Registered NGOs get an alert and arrange collection.
  6. Impact is recorded
    Sales, donations and food rescued, tracked separately.

Impact so far

Food safety comes first

Businesses must give accurate storage and handling details and confirm the food is safe. Expired or withdrawn listings disappear automatically, and food that cannot be stored safely is never relisted. AI may flag missing information, but it never declares food safe.
